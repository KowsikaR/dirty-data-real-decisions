# Operational answers

## Question 1 — Have case closure times increased over the years?

**Answer: increased. Confidence: moderate.** The comparison uses median closure duration among valid, deduplicated records by intake year. This is descriptive evidence and does not establish a cause.

## Question 2 — Which case categories take longest to close?

**Answer: Account. Confidence: moderate.** It has the highest median duration (24.0 days) among categories with valid durations. Small groups should be treated cautiously.

## Question 3 — What caused the increase in closure time?

**Cannot be answered reliably from the available data. Confidence: high.** The export contains dates, identifiers, and categories, but no staffing, workload/backlog, priority, policy, process-change, or channel variables. The data can show that a change occurred; it cannot identify its operational cause.

### What would be needed

Staffing and queue history, incoming volume, priority/SLA, workflow timestamps, policy/process-change dates, and a stable case-level audit history.
